# ResRef Product Brief

## What ResRef Is

ResRef is a medical and academic research reference platform. It combines research discovery, practical research tools, expert consultation, academic opportunities, and account-based workspaces into one web application.

The current implementation is a React frontend with a Django backend. The public website is served through the React app, while Django provides content, account, dashboard, contact, service, and research-tool APIs.

## Main Product Areas

- Public research platform: Home, database, questionnaires, article/content pages, about, FAQ, contact, legal pages.
- Research database: Searchable public research resources, posts, categories, tags, authors, and dynamic content.
- Research tools: Reference generator, sample size calculator, journal finder, advanced search, questionnaires library.
- Expert services: Expert consultation request flow and service request handling.
- Community and opportunities: Research opportunities and join-our-team application flows.
- User accounts: Registration, sign in, Google sign in, profile, membership state, saved tool workspaces.
- Admin dashboard: Protected Django-session dashboard for users, posts, media, forms, contacts, services, opportunities, settings, and reports.

## Target Users

- Medical students who need simple research guidance, references, tools, and learning resources.
- Doctors and clinicians planning studies, audits, papers, or academic projects.
- Researchers and academic teams who need organized resources, collaboration paths, and writing support.
- Early-career academics who need expert help with study design, analysis, manuscripts, and journal selection.
- Internal ResRef team members who manage content, contacts, services, opportunities, and platform operations.

## Problems ResRef Solves

- Research resources are scattered across websites, documents, databases, and informal channels.
- Students and clinicians often do not know which research tool or workflow to use.
- Referencing, sample size planning, journal matching, and research support are time-consuming.
- Academic service requests need structured intake, routing, and follow-up.
- Platform administrators need one place to manage content, forms, users, media, and service workflows.

## Current Value Proposition

ResRef helps students, doctors, researchers, and academic teams find reliable resources, plan studies, prepare references, choose journals, and request expert support through a single research-focused platform.

## Future Vision

ResRef can grow from a research support website into a global research ecosystem:

- A trusted research knowledge hub for healthcare and academic audiences.
- A guided research workflow platform from idea to publication.
- A marketplace or service layer for expert consultation, manuscript support, methodology, statistics, and publication planning.
- A personal research workspace with saved calculations, references, forms, and learning history.
- A mobile-first companion app for students and researchers.
- A scalable admin and operations platform for content, services, teams, and analytics.

## Product Positioning Direction

The platform should feel like a premium international research, education, and healthcare technology company. It should be more structured and trustworthy than a student website, more usable than a WordPress content site, and more research-specific than a generic SaaS template.
